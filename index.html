⋆.˚ The Art of my Soul ⋆.˚

I have done many types of art, and naturally, I gravitated towards certain mediums, and shyed away from others. I now focus my craft on Digital Character Art, and Graffiti in my free time.

Digital Characters have made my life so much better in ways I can't begin to explain. It helps to express my feelings and emotions through stories in my characters. Additionally, I wish to break into the gaming industry and show more diversity in terms of body sizes, race, sexuality, and even representation for disabilities, visible or not.
As a child, I never saw South-Asian representation in any gaming or "nerdy" media which made me upset and confused. I never had anyone to relate to all my life, despite living in such a diverse city like New York. Instead, many kids were racist to me, and it really lasted and left a mark on my life. Creating these characters really has helped me heal and also help represent people that may feel similar in their own body.
Sxahan is my primary persona, but despite that, they don't have any story to go with their design. The only thing that matters is how iconic they are. However, I do plan on giving them a story. I just don't know if I should stick to my real life story or not. I do have a headcannon that they are just me in real life, except has powers that allow them to explore different universes and shapeshift. It took me a long time to find a design I was comfortable with. In the end I realized I was overthinking it and just thought of my aura. I absolutely love poofy, expressive hair, so what I did was give Sxahan huge, spiky, neon pink hair.


This is one of the only pieces in which I drew Sxahan, on the left, in their complete outfit, but now I'm debating on if I should give them a skater outfit, since it has taken up so much of my life recently.
Graffiti has also been a more recent exploration for me. As of October of 2026, I've been going through a difficult time with my family, so simply interacting with old places, and seeing and contributing to the graffiti makes me feel as if I'm a part of a bigger community, despite my surroundings being so quiet. It's very comforting, not to mention that I can skate in nearby areas. Roller-skating is very theraputic for me and can contribute to my art creation as it gives me time to think and brainstorm. Staying in my thoughts while I skate gives my ideas time to develop and reflect.
Speaking of, I'm also learning how to skateboard. My best friend and my boyfriend both can skateboard to some extent, so it'd make sense for me to learn (and aura-farm while I'm at it...) too.
